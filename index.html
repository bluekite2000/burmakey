Why is Burglish a problem?

Burglish is fast. But 2 of every 3 Burglish words look exactly like a different word — taja alone can be တရား (justice), တစ်ရာ (a hundred) or တာယာ (a tyre). Your friend has to guess. Usually right. Sometimes wrong.

Here you type the same way, tap the word you meant, and your friend gets real Burmese script. No guessing.

Your words stay yours

Everything — the dictionary, the prediction, the learning — runs on your device. Your messages are never sent anywhere. If you choose to send feedback, we receive numbers (taps per word, how often the first suggestion was right), never your text. In Myanmar that isn't a feature. It's the only acceptable design.

Help make it real

We built this from books and data. Now it needs your eyes: which words come out wrong? Which spellings feel weird? Your opinion is worth more than another month of our work.

Got 20 minutes? The guided test helps us most: copy some sentences, try a few tricky ones, then write anything you like. It shows us how you really spell Burglish.

Privacy

Everything runs on your phone. Your messages are never sent anywhere. If you choose to help us, we get numbers only — like how many taps you needed. Never your words.

Two extras you can switch on if you want — both off by default, and the keyboard asks you first:

  • Words it didn't know — just those words, never your sentence.
  • What you typed and picked — this is what makes the dictionary better for everyone.

In Myanmar, keeping everything on your phone is not a bonus feature. It is the only right way to build this.

Why is Burglish a problem?

Because it works just well enough. Typing khaw-style toneless Latin is faster than hunting through a script keyboard — but in real chat, 2 of every 3 Burglish words are spelled exactly like a different Burmese word. Your reader guesses. Usually right. Sometimes wrong. This keyboard keeps the typing you already know and removes the guessing: you pick the real word, your friend receives real Burmese script.

Your words stay yours

Everything — the dictionary, the prediction, the learning — runs on your device. Your messages are never sent anywhere. If you choose to send feedback, we receive numbers (taps per word, how often the first suggestion was right), never your text. In Myanmar that isn't a feature. It's the only acceptable design.

Help make it real

This was built from open data and simulation. What it needs now is Burmese eyes: try it, tell us which words come out wrong, which spellings feel natural, what's missing. Two minutes of your opinion is worth more than another month of our engineering.
